Privacy Policy

InSight Fencing ("the app") is a training, journal and referee app for fencers. We built it so that everything you enter stays on your own iPhone. This page explains what that means.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or share any of your information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your iPhone

Your drill scores, journal entries, competition and pool records, checklists, referee bouts and achievements are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, this data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own Apple account.

Camera and photos

If you choose to scan a pool sheet, the app asks for permission to use the camera, or lets you pick a photo you already have. The picture is read on your iPhone (using Apple's on-device text recognition) only to fill in fencers' names. It is not uploaded anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can schedule gentle reminders on your iPhone about unfinished preparation items before a tournament. These reminders are created on your device and are not sent through any server.

Children

The app is suitable for fencers of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
