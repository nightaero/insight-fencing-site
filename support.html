Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

Do I need a camera to track a pool?

No. You can type the fencers' names in instead of scanning the pool sheet.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restoring your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one brings the app's data with it.

How do I see the welcome tour again?

On the Drills tab, open “How to use InSight” and tap “Watch the welcome tour again”. The same page explains each part of the app.

Do I need to pay for anything?

No. InSight Fencing is free, and Sparks are earned only by using the app. There are no real-money purchases.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual entries inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.
