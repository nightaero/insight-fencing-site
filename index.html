InSight Fencing

A friendly iPhone app for fencers: hand-eye coordination drills, a bout journal, competition preparation and pool tracking, and referee tools.

Support

Have a question, found a bug, or have an idea? Email contact@altaerislabs.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

Do I need a camera to track a pool? No. You can type the fencers' names in instead of scanning the pool sheet.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone? Restoring your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one brings the app's data with it.
