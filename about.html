InSight Fencing

Train, track and have fun.

A friendly iPhone companion for fencers of every age and level. Sharpen your reactions with quick visual drills, keep a journal of your bouts, get ready for competitions and referee practice matches, all in one place.

Free on iPhone iOS 17 or later Foil · Épée · Sabre
What's inside

Four tools, one friendly app

Each part of the app has its own cartoon buddy and is built to be quick to use, whether you're at home, at practice or between bouts at a tournament.

Drills

Reaction Light, Distance Game, Eagle Eye and Parry Lines train hand-eye coordination, timing and focus. Charts show how you improve.

Journal

Log practice and competition bouts, rate how you felt and note what worked. The quick practice log saves several bouts at once.

Competition

Packing checklist, tournament countdowns and pool tracking. Snap the pool sheet and see when you're up, on deck or in the hole.

Referee

Big score buttons, a bout clock with periods and priority, cards and undo, plus a Rules Academy to learn the rules step by step.

A closer look

Made to be picked up and used

Bright, simple screens with big buttons, so it works just as well on a small phone in a busy fencing hall.

And a little extra

Reasons to keep coming back

Small rewards and calm moments that make practice feel good, without any pressure.

Your own fencer

Design a fencer with your own hair, colours and kit, and watch them cheer when you do well.

Sparks & store

Earn Sparks from drills and journal entries, then spend them on new kits. No real money, ever.

Achievements

Trophies, streaks and a sticker book of cartoon cities from around the world.

Calm breathing

Short, guided breathing to settle nerves before a bout or a big day.

Today's challenge

A fresh mini goal every day to keep training interesting.

Easy to learn

A short welcome tour and a "How to use InSight" guide help new fencers find their way.
